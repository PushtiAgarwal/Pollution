Air Pollution

Air pollution is the contamination of air by harmful substances. It occurs when gases, dust, or smoke enter the atmosphere. These pollutants make the air unsafe for humans, animals, and plants. Common pollutants include carbon monoxide, sulfur dioxide, and nitrogen oxides.

Factories, vehicles, and burning fossil fuels are major sources of air pollution. Deforestation and construction activities also add harmful particles to the air. Air pollution can appear as visible smog or be invisible like toxic gases.

It causes serious health problems such as asthma, lung diseases, and heart issues. Children and elderly people are more vulnerable to polluted air. Polluted air can also damage crops, forests, and water bodies.

Air pollution worsens climate change by trapping heat in the atmosphere. It also leads to acid rain, harming soil and aquatic life. Indoor air pollution from unsafe cooking and heating affects millions globally.

Reducing emissions, using clean energy, and planting trees can help fight air pollution. Clean air is essential for good health, environmental balance, and a sustainable future.
